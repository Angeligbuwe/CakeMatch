# CakeMatch

A cake planning website that recommends a suitable cake size based on the number of guests and the customer's  preferences.

## User Problem

Customers may not know which cake size will serve their guests or which design will suit their occasion. They may also forget to provide important information when planning a customsied cake. 

## How CakeMatch Works (MVP - Minimum Viable Product)

1. The customer selects the celebration type.
2. The customer enters the number of guests.
3. The customer chooses a preferred cake flavour and design.
4. CakeMatch recommends a suitable cake size.
5. The website displays an order summary.

## Future Features

Future versions may include estimated prices, dietary requirements, event dates, image uploads, customer reviews, delivery or collection options, WhatsApp contact, and light and dark mode.
